# Жизнь в деревне: практика (слова + грамматика)

## Диалог

**Nick:** I love the **countryside**. Our **village** is small, but life here is
**quieter** than in the city.
**Lena:** Really? I think city life is more interesting.
**Nick:** Maybe, but the **fresh air** here is **cleaner** than in a **crowded** city.
And my **neighbour** has a **farm** with lots of **animals**.
**Lena:** I like that village life is **more peaceful** than **noisy** city life.
**Nick:** Yes, and life on a farm is **better** for animals than life in the city.

## Мини-практика

1. Что Ник любит в деревне? — **countryside, тихую и спокойную жизнь**.
2. Переведи слово neighbour — **сосед**.
3. Составь сравнение: village life / peaceful / city life — **Village life is more peaceful than city life.**
4. Впиши сравнительную форму: The air here is ___ (clean) than in a crowded city. — **cleaner**.
5. Переведи слово crowded — **многолюдный**.
6. Впиши исключение: Life on a farm is ___ (good) for animals. — **better**.
